What to try once a field is loaded

Blink
Play the frames. A nearby star or a Solar System body jumps from one sky pass to the next.
− Median
Subtract the median of all frames. Everything that stays put disappears; what moved or changed remains.
Spectrum
Each exposure sees the crosshair at one wavelength, so together they give its 0.75–5.0 µm spectrum and a light curve.
Overlays
Gaia DR3 and CatWISE sources, moved by their proper motion to the date of each frame.
Link
Every view is a URL. The same parameters return a PNG, a GIF or the measurements.
